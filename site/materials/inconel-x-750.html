Where it is specified

  • Springs, fasteners and rotor components to roughly 700 °C
  • Excellent relaxation resistance under sustained load
  • Properties depend on a controlled ageing cycle

An under-aged spring relaxes in service and tests fine cold

Like 718, X-750 is a heat-treatment grade. Chemistry inside limits proves nothing about whether the ageing cycle was run correctly, and an under-aged spring relaxes in service.

What we specify and check

  • Heat treatment records demanded and read
  • Hardness verification on the delivered heat
  • PMI for Ti and Al against B637

We commission an accredited independent laboratory to test the heat before the material leaves China. You will receive that report unedited, whether it passes or not.
