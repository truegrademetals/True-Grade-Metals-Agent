Where it is specified

  • Sulphuric and phosphoric acid service
  • Good resistance to chloride pitting and stress-corrosion cracking
  • Low carbon gives good as-welded performance

Diluted on nickel, because nickel is most of what it costs

904L is the natural substitute to offer against an Incoloy 825 order, and nickel is most of what it costs, so nickel is where the dilution pressure sits. Copper below the 1.0 % floor removes the sulphuric acid resistance that is the reason to choose it.

What we specify and check

  • PMI reading nickel against the 23.0–28.0 % window
  • Copper verified against the 1.0–2.0 % window
  • Carbon verified against the 0.020 % maximum
  • Confirmation the order is 904L and not a substituted 825 requirement

We commission an accredited independent laboratory to test the heat before the material leaves China. You will receive that report unedited, whether it passes or not.
