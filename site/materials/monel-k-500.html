Where it is specified

  • Pump shafts, propeller shafts, fasteners and valve stems in marine service
  • Strength of a structural alloy with the corrosion behaviour of Monel 400
  • Non-magnetic and spark-resistant

Over-ageing buys strength and hydrogen embrittlement together

K-500 is vulnerable to hydrogen embrittlement if over-aged or over-hardened, which makes the heat treatment record and the hardness result the safety-critical documents. Aluminium below the 2.30 % floor means the age hardening cannot reach specified strength.

What we specify and check

  • Hardness verification on the delivered material
  • Heat treatment records demanded and read
  • PMI for Al and Ti against B865

We commission an accredited independent laboratory to test the heat before the material leaves China. You will receive that report unedited, whether it passes or not.
