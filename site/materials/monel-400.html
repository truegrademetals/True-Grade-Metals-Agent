Where it is specified

  • Seawater, brackish water and marine service — resists erosion and cavitation
  • Hydrofluoric acid, alkalis, and reducing conditions
  • Retains strength and toughness down to cryogenic temperatures

Nickel costs four times copper, and the ratio is the alloy

Nickel is roughly four times the price of copper, so the economic incentive runs one way: push nickel toward the 63 % floor and copper toward the 34 % ceiling. A heat sitting outside the ratio still looks like Monel and still machines like Monel, and it corrodes faster in exactly the seawater service it was bought for.

What we specify and check

  • PMI specified on the delivered heat, reading the Ni:Cu ratio rather than nickel alone
  • Iron verified against the 2.5 % maximum
  • Sulphur checked where hot working or welding is planned
  • Certificate matched to the heat number in the crate

We commission an accredited independent laboratory to test the heat before the material leaves China. You will receive that report unedited, whether it passes or not.
