Where it is specified

  • Oxidising and mixed acid service where C-276 is marginal
  • Pharmaceutical and chemical process vessels, scrubbers, flue gas systems
  • Outstanding resistance to pitting and crevice corrosion

C-22 and C-276 are cross-sold in both directions

C-22 and C-276 are easy to cross-sell in either direction: the two look identical in the crate, and their certificates read alike to anyone not checking the numbers. The alloys are not interchangeable: their chromium and molybdenum balance is different, and so is the service each one survives.

What we specify and check

  • PMI distinguishing Cr 20.0–22.5 and Mo 12.5–14.5 from the C-276 windows
  • Certificate matched to the heat number in the crate
  • ASTM G28 testing where specified
  • Confirmation the grade ordered is the grade quoted, in writing, before the PO

We commission an accredited independent laboratory to test the heat before the material leaves China. You will receive that report unedited, whether it passes or not.
