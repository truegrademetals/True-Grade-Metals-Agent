Where it is specified

  • Gas turbine combustion components, ducting and burner cans
  • Oxidation resistance to roughly 1200 °C
  • Good strength retention under sustained high temperature

Carbon sits in a band, so a chemistry check against a maximum passes it

Carbon on Hastelloy X sits in a band rather than under a ceiling, because the alloy needs it for high-temperature strength. A heat below 0.05 % will not perform at temperature even though a chemistry check against a maximum would pass it.

What we specify and check

  • Carbon verified against the 0.05–0.15 % band, not a maximum
  • PMI for Cr, Mo and Fe against B435
  • Solution anneal records demanded

We commission an accredited independent laboratory to test the heat before the material leaves China. You will receive that report unedited, whether it passes or not.
