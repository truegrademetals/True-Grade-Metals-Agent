Where it is specified

  • Seawater systems, desalination, offshore process piping
  • Chloride-bearing service where austenitic grades crack
  • PREN typically 42 or above

Duplex fails on structure while the chemistry still reads clean

Duplex grades fail on structure, not chemistry. Incorrect solution annealing or an uncontrolled cool leaves intermetallic sigma phase and a ferrite balance outside 35–55 %, which destroys toughness and corrosion resistance while every element on the certificate still reads in range. Nitrogen at the low end also quietly drops the PREN below what was specified.

What we specify and check

  • Ferrite content measured on the delivered material
  • PREN calculated from measured values, not from the nominal grade
  • Solution anneal and quench records demanded
  • ASTM A923 testing for intermetallic phase where specified
  • Impact testing where low-temperature service applies

We commission an accredited independent laboratory to test the heat before the material leaves China. You will receive that report unedited, whether it passes or not.
