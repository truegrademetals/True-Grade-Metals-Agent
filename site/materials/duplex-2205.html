The workhorse duplex stainless: roughly twice the yield strength of 316L with far better chloride stress-corrosion cracking resistance, at a lower nickel cost.

Where it is specified

  • Process piping, pressure vessels and tanks in chloride service
  • Marine, desalination, oil and gas gathering systems
  • PREN typically 34 or above

The wider S31803 windows still ship against 2205 orders

The older S31803 designation has wider composition windows than S32205 and is still supplied against 2205 orders. As with all duplex, the real risk is structural: a bad anneal leaves sigma phase and a ferrite balance out of range while the chemistry certificate reads perfectly.

What we specify and check

  • Confirmation of S32205 rather than the wider S31803 windows
  • Ferrite content measured on the delivered material
  • PREN calculated from measured values
  • ASTM A923 testing for intermetallic phase where specified

We commission an accredited independent laboratory to test the heat before the material leaves China. You will receive that report unedited, whether it passes or not.
